### 🎹 Piano

<div align="center">
<img src="https://placehold.co/250x170/1a1b27/7aa2f7?text=Piano+1&font=montserrat" alt="Piano placeholder 1" width="250" />

Sitting down at the piano is my favourite way to switch off and recharge. There is something magical about turning dots on a page into sound, and about the patience it takes to polish a single difficult passage until it flows. Music teaches me discipline, listening and creativity, and I'm convinced it makes me a better engineer too.

### 🧠 Philosophy

<div align="center">
<img src="https://placehold.co/250x170/1a1b27/bb9af7?text=Philosophy+1&font=montserrat" alt="Philosophy placeholder 1" width="250" />

<br/>

I love asking the big questions: what is knowledge, what makes a good life, and how should we deal with the technology we build? Reading and debating philosophy sharpens my thinking, keeps me humble and reminds me to look at problems from more than one angle. Good engineering often starts with asking *why* before asking *how*.
